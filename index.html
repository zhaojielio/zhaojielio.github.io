01 / About

跨越学科，
追问心智。Across disciplines.
Toward the mind.

我的学习路径从跨文化语言交流延伸至计算机系统与认知科学。我关注语言、大脑和智能机器的交汇处，希望通过能够被检验的计算模型，理解认知与学习。My academic path connects intercultural linguistic mediation, computing systems, and cognitive science. I am interested in where language, brains, and intelligent machines meet — and in computational accounts precise enough to test.

严谨的想法、清晰的代码，以及能够跨越学科边界的解释，是我希望持续打磨的工作方式。I value rigorous ideas, readable code, and explanations that travel well across disciplines.

正在探索的
问题。Questions
in focus.

从神经信号到语言与行动，探索生物智能与人工智能之间的联系。From neural signals to language and action: exploring connections between biological and artificial intelligence.

01神经解码Neural decodingEEG / MEG / BRAIN–COMPUTER INTERFACES+

如何从神经信号中解码感知与认知，并将这些方法用于更稳健的脑机接口？How can perception and cognition be decoded from neural signals, with a view toward more robust brain–computer interfaces?

02世界模型World modelsNEUROAI / PREDICTION / GENERALIZATION+

内部模型如何支持生物与人工智能体的预测、规划和泛化？How do internal models support prediction, planning, and generalization in biological and artificial agents?

03约束下的语言生成Language under constraintsNLP / LYRIC GENERATION / CREATIVE CONTROL+

如何在歌词生成中协调结构、语义与韵律，使计算方法支持可控的创作？How can computational approaches to lyric generation reconcile structure, meaning, and rhythm while supporting creative control?

04具身智能Embodied intelligenceNEUROROBOTICS / PERCEPTION / LEARNING+

如何将神经计算与智能体的感知、行动和环境交互中的学习联系起来？How can neural computation connect to agents that perceive, act, and learn through interaction with the world?
